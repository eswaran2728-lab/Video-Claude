# Editing workflow

## Intake and asset inventory
Infer a reasonable first draft from supplied material. Ask only for blocking missing assets or facts. Record purpose, audience, aspect ratio, duration, language, exact titles, logo, footage permission and music status. An example project is a schema illustration, never evidence of an actual event. Preserve original filenames in an inventory and use separate proxies.

## Speech-led edit
Transcribe with word or segment timestamps. Mark clean takes, false starts, repeated ideas, quiet gaps and factual claims. Propose a hook drawn from actual speech; do not manufacture a quote by combining unrelated sentences. Keep a source-to-output map. Leave 80–150 ms breathing room around natural phrases as a starting point. Inspect joins for clipped consonants. Use J/L cuts where the available renderer supports separate audio and video timing. Add b-roll to explain an idea, not just hide every cut.

## Photo-led edit
Inspect every image before ordering it. Use a coherent arc: establishing context, key people, interactions, memorable details, group closing. Hold important group photos for 2.5–4 seconds. Use modest 1.00–1.05 moves and intentional focal anchors. Fit the entire frame when cropping would remove people or logos. Do not generate absent guests or alter the significance of a meeting.

## Timeline contract
Create `edit-plan.json` with source paths, source in/out, output start/end, rationale, captions, effects, audio and export parameters. Use explicit absolute paths resolved locally. Source ranges must be within media duration. A slowed clip needs a matching audio strategy. Recompute all output timing after removing sections. Never reuse old subtitle timestamps after a ripple edit.

## Creative pass
Choose one base style and at most 2–3 effect families. Reserve strong motion for hooks, transitions and genuine emphasis. Build rhythm from information changes; do not force cuts onto every beat. Keep the opening readable without sound and dialogue understandable without graphics.

## Preview and revision
Render a low-resolution preview and inspect beginning, middle, ending and every advanced effect. Log exactly which assets and timings were changed. For revisions, retain the prior version, apply requested deltas and recheck dependent captions, music and duration. Obtain preview approval before final export by default.

## Deliverables
Return the preview or approved final, editable project, edit decision list, asset inventory and a concise note on substitutions. Do not promise engagement results. Keep posting separate from export.
