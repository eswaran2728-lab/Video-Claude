# Style recipes

These are original suggested parameters, not extracted vendor settings. All styles are available to anyone; the sales-page gender groupings are not restrictions.

## Chalk Talk
- Use for: teaching, coaching, demonstrations.
- Palette: background `#111820`, text `#F4EBDD`, accent `#F3CB56`.
- Typography: handwritten display plus plain sans; 56–76 px headings and 46–58 px captions at 1080 × 1920.
- Starting shot duration: 2–4 seconds; preserve speech meaning over tempo.
- Composition and treatment: Use a dark board-like field with a medium shot and a reserved diagram area. Reveal one diagram stroke with each explanation. Keep handwriting to titles; subtitles remain clean.
- Candidate effects: Doodle Boil, Checklist; begin with one accent effect per 6–10 seconds.
- Motion: 200–350 ms eased entrances; 120–200 ms exits.
- QA: compare skin tones with the source, inspect caption contrast and mobile readability, and remove effects that compete with the subject.
