# Carousel recipes

Original interpretations. Start with 1080 × 1350 px slides and 90 px margins; confirm destination requirements before export. Draft the full slide copy and receive approval before designing. Use 6–8 slides by default: hook, problem, 3–4 useful points, recap and call to action. Give each slide one purpose. Keep body copy under roughly 35 words and test at phone size. Use supplied images or explicitly authorised generated assets. Export numbered PNGs and a contact sheet; preserve editable layouts.

## Horizon
Background `#101727`, accent `#FF9A4D`. Use a subtle horizon glow under a cutout or relevant scene; let the heading occupy the upper half.
Use 72–100 px titles and 34–44 px body text as initial settings. Keep slide numbers consistent. On information-heavy slides, reduce copy before reducing type. Preview the opening, one middle slide and the closing slide together to check continuity.
