# 20 original caption presets

These are our own presets. The public page does not disclose its caption style names. Start at 46–58 px for Latin captions and 50–62 px for Tamil at 1080 × 1920. Limit to two lines and about 3–7 spoken words per chunk; prefer meaningful clauses over arbitrary fixed lengths. Avoid splitting Tamil grapheme clusters. Shape text with a Tamil-capable engine and visually inspect complex characters. Aim for sufficient reading time, approximately 12–18 characters per second where language and speech permit; never retime speech solely to meet that heuristic.
