# Event, Tamil and product workflows

## Silambam meeting or lunch montage
Use the user's photos to establish the venue, introduce people, show conversation and shared lunch, then close with a group photo. A 30–45 second first draft can use 10–16 carefully selected images rather than every duplicate. Keep important faces visible; group photos need longer holds. Use restrained gold and dark tones for formal moments and warmer imagery for lunch. Do not add facts absent from the brief.

Exact supplied branding text: `Mahaguru Sri S Arumugam`. Confirm any Tamil rendering with the user rather than inventing a transliteration. For an event involving Persatuan Silambam Malaysia Daerah Sepang and Karate R. Tiagarajan, verify the exact association and guest spelling before final titles. Do not assume a meeting implies an endorsement.

Keep the supplied transparent logo visible throughout if requested, approximately 100–140 px wide at 1080 px frame width, inside the safe region. Inspect both bright and dark photos; add a subtle backing only if needed. Never recreate the logo from memory. If the logo is absent, mark that dependency and preview without a fabricated substitute.

Suggested original storyboard: 0–3 s strong group image and short context title; 3–10 s arrival and greeting; 10–22 s conversation; 22–33 s lunch and candid moments; 33–40 s group close with a brief thanks. Adjust all durations to the actual supplied images and chosen music.

## Multilingual text
Use exact approved English, Malay and Tamil text. Keep names in the user's preferred spelling. Use a Tamil-capable font with a licence that allows the intended output. Render a glyph test including the actual strings; inspect vowel signs and line breaks. Prefer one primary caption language with an optional smaller translation, rather than three dense lines. Avoid character-by-character Tamil typing unless the renderer respects grapheme clusters and shaping.

## Eshan Creation product reel
Use actual supplied product shots. Show a clean opening hero, detail close-ups, scale reference and approved price/contact card. Do not infer dimensions, material, stock or discounts from an image. Represent colour and painted finishes accurately; note lighting-dependent appearance if relevant. Use 20–30 seconds initially with slower holds on detailed statues. Keep measurements and prices out until confirmed. An original suggested palette is charcoal, cream and muted gold; it is editable rather than a claimed official brand specification.
