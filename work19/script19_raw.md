### Slide 1 — FOCA Checklist
This training shows you how to fill in, sign, and save a FOCA checklist on your phone. Security fills in the checklist first. Then the Airline Representative signs. Then the Security Representative signs and saves it. Both representatives use the same phone. We’ll go through it one step at a time.
### Slide 2 — Who does what
There are three parts. First, Security staff fill in the checklist. Second, the Airline Representative checks the details, types their own name and ID, and signs. Third, the Security Representative types their own name and ID, signs, and saves the record.
### Slide 3 — Rules for every FOCA
Use your AirAsia Google account. Your email address should end in airasia.com. When it’s your turn to sign, type your own name and ID. Sign for yourself. Never sign for someone else. Start a new form for every new case.
### Slide 4 — Get the app link
Ask your supervisor for the app link. You can also use the QR code shown here. Open your phone camera, point it at the QR code, and tap the link that appears.
@ Point to the QR code and pause.
### Slide 5 — Open the app in Chrome
Open Chrome on your phone. Paste the link into the address bar. If you scanned the QR code, open the link in Chrome. If Google asks you to sign in, choose your AirAsia account. Wait until you see four forms on the screen.
If you want to open the app with one tap next time, tap the three dots in Chrome and choose Add to Home screen.
### Slide 6 — Tap FOCA
Look for the card that says FOCA. Check the code below the name. It should say AA/SEC/F/070. Once you see the right code, tap the card.
@ Point to the form name and code before tapping.
### Slide 7 — Look at the whole form
At the top, you’ll see boxes for the flight details. In the middle, you’ll see the checklist. At the bottom, you’ll see a blue Next button. Scroll down so you can see where each part is. Then go back to the top to start.
### Slide 8 — Fill in the flight details
Start with the flight number. Then enter the aircraft registration, the destination, and the date.
Next, enter the arrival and departure times. STA is the scheduled arrival time. ATA is the actual arrival time. STD is the scheduled departure time. ATD is the actual departure time. Finally, enter the aircraft bay.
The flight number and date are required. Check both before you move on. The slide shows test data. For a real case, enter the details for that flight.
@ Point to each box as you name it.
### Slide 9 — Tick one box on every line
Look for Areas of Coverage. Read the first line. If you completed that check, tap Completed. If the area was not checked, tap Not Checked. Add a remark if you need to explain what happened.
Now move to the next line. Choose one answer on every line.
@ Show one example, then ask trainees to point to the next line.
### Slide 10 — Complete Additional Services
Scroll down to Additional Services. Read each line. Choose Required/Completed if the service was required and completed. Choose Not Applicable if the service does not apply to this case.
Once you’ve checked your answers, tap the blue Next button.
### Slide 11 — Hand the phone to the Airline Representative
Security staff, hand the phone to the Airline Representative. Once the Airline Representative is holding the phone, they tap the orange button that says Airline Representative has the phone.
@ Show the physical handover before pointing to the orange button.
### Slide 12 — Airline Representative checks the form
Airline Representative, read through the details on the screen. If you spot a mistake, tap Something is wrong. Then give the phone back to Security so they can correct it.
If everything is correct, move on to the name and ID boxes.
### Slide 13 — Airline Representative enters their name and ID
Tap the name box and type your own name. Then tap the ID box and enter your own staff ID or staff number. Look at both boxes again before you sign.
### Slide 14 — Airline Representative signs
Use your finger or a stylus to sign inside the box. The Airline Representative signs for themselves. Security staff, give them time to complete their signature.
### Slide 15 — Clear a signature mistake
If you make a mistake, tap Clear. The signature box will become empty. Sign again in the same box.
@ Show what happens when you tap Clear.
### Slide 16 — Confirm the Airline Representative’s signature
When you’re happy with your signature, tap Confirm my signature. The next screen will ask you to hand the phone back to Security.
### Slide 17 — Hand the phone back to Security
When you see Hand the phone back to SECURITY STAFF, pass the phone back to the Security Representative. Once the Security Representative is holding it, they tap the blue button that says Security staff has the phone.
### Slide 18 — Security Representative enters their name and ID
Security Representative, tap the name box and type your own name. Then enter your own staff ID or staff number. Check both boxes before you sign.
### Slide 19 — Security Representative signs
Use your finger or a stylus to sign inside the box. If you make a mistake, tap Clear and sign again. Check that your signature is visible in the box.
### Slide 20 — Save the record
Find the green button that says Save record (PDF to Drive + Sheet). Tap it once. When it says Saving..., keep the app open and wait.
@ Point to the green button, then pause on the saving screen.
### Slide 21 — Wait for “Saved”
Look for three things. A tick, the word Saved, and a Record ID. The Record ID is the reference number for this case.
Once you see Saved, the app has put the signed checklist in Google Drive and added one row to Google Sheets. That row has a link to the signed PDF.
### Slide 22 — If the screen says “Not saved”
If you see Not saved, stay on the same screen. Your answers and signatures are still there. Check your mobile data or Wi-Fi. Then tap Save again and wait for the confirmation.
@ Ask trainees what they should do if they see Not saved. Let them say the steps back to you.
### Slide 23 — Open the completed PDF
On the Saved screen, tap Open the completed PDF. This opens the signed checklist in Google Drive.
### Slide 24 — Check the completed PDF
First, check the file name. Look for the form name, the date, and the Record ID. Then scroll through every page. Find the Airline Representative’s signature. Then find the Security Representative’s signature. Make sure you can see both.
### Slide 25 — Open the sharing menu
If you need to send the PDF link to an authorised person, look at the top right of the screen. Tap the blue Share button.
### Slide 26 — Copy the restricted link
Check that access is set to Restricted. Keep it on Restricted. Then tap Copy link and send the link only to an authorised person.
### Slide 27 — Where the checklist and signatures go
@ Point to each folder on the slide as you name it.
The app saves one PDF for each case. That PDF contains the completed checklist and both signatures.
Here’s where authorised staff can find it. In Google Drive, open My Drive. Then open AA-SEC Signed Forms (RESTRICTED). Inside that folder, open FOCA.
The file name starts with FOCA and includes the flight number, the date, and the Record ID. If you’ve just saved the case, you can open the same file straight from the Saved screen by tapping Open the completed PDF.
### Slide 28 — Where the record details go
@ Point to the sample row, then point to the PDF link in that row.
The app also adds one row to the FOCA Records Google Sheet. The sheet is in the same FOCA folder as the PDFs.
Each row shows the Record ID, the date and time it was saved in Malaysia time, the flight number, the names of both representatives, and a link to the signed PDF.
If an authorised person needs to find the checklist later, they can look for the Record ID in the sheet and tap the PDF link on that row. The app writes the row automatically. Leave the rows as they are.
### Slide 29 — Start a new case
When you have a different case to record, tap Start a new FOCA form. Enter the details for that case. Once you save it, it gets its own Record ID, its own signed PDF, and its own row in FOCA Records.
### Slide 30 — Final check
Before you finish, ask yourself five questions.
One. Are the flight details and date correct?
Two. Did I choose one answer on every checklist line?
Three. Did the Airline Representative enter their own name and ID and sign?
Four. Did the Security Representative enter their own name and ID and sign?
Five. Did I see Saved and check both signatures in the PDF?
