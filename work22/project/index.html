Service history is usually scattered across workshops and
paper receipts.
RevLog keeps one record for every bike.
The Rider owns it, and workshops add to it with the
Rider's approval.
A bike with no owner yet can be served too.
First, pick one: I ride, or I run a workshop.
Then sign in with Google or email.
There are four roles.
The Rider owns the record.
The Workshop owner runs the workshop.
Staff save each service.
And the Admin approves workshops and checks ownership.
A Rider finds their bike, then adds the odometer and plate.
Two quick questions help RevLog tell you what's due.
A short guide follows.
Then the Rider agrees to how their data is used.
The bike is now in the Garage.
Below it: Log service, Verify this bike, and What's due.
Verify this bike asks for photos of your driving licence and geran.
They prove the bike is yours.
Document photos are deleted after the check,
or kept up to 14 days if checked by hand.
Once the bike is verified, My code becomes available.
We'll see the Admin's check later.
What's due lists every item. Tap one to see how far it has left.
Log service records your own work.
It appears in History as a Rider entry,
with its details.
Fuel tracks fill-ups and kilometres per litre.
Plus a service schedule, renewal reminders, and spending.
A Workshop owner registers the workshop.
RevLog checks every workshop before it can search bikes.
The Admin approves it,
and now the workshop can search by plate.
Workshop mode has Search, Customers,
Packages and Team.
Packages save the usual items and costs,
ready in one tap.
Under Team, the owner adds Branches.
The Free plan allows one Branch.
The owner creates an invite code, and a Staff member joins with it.
The Free plan limits how many staff you can add.
Now, a workshop visit. Staff search the plate.
The bike belongs to a Rider: Approval needed.
The Rider opens My code: a QR and a 6-digit code,
valid for ten minutes, used once.
Staff scan it,
or type the code and tap Done.
The bike opens, with the Rider's first name and the
workshop's record.
In the Rider's History, the workshop's record appears with
the Workshop badge.
The Rider typed nothing.
A plate not in the system? Staff tap Add this bike,
with its brand and model.
Then a package, the odometer and costs,
and Save record.
The record carries the workshop's stamp,
and Staff show the Claim QR.
The Rider scans the Claim QR,
and photographs the geran.
Once ownership is confirmed, the bike and the workshop's
records are theirs.
The Rider sees which workshops have access,
and can remove one at any time.
There are two ways to sell.
Sell to a RevLog rider: the seller types the buyer's Receive code.
Or Transfer to a buyer: a locked file,
opened with a 6-digit code.
A bike checked by hand waits as not yet verified.
The Admin opens the case, compares the names,
and approves it.
After approval, My code appears where Verify this bike was.
The Admin also sets plans and limits:
on the Free plan, 30 bikes and one Branch.
A workshop sees your bike only after you approve it.
Document photos are deleted after the check,
or kept up to 14 days if checked by hand.
One record for every bike: owned by the Rider,
added to by workshops, checked by the Admin.
