**Slide 1**
Welcome. This guide shows you how to use the four AA-SEC signing forms on your phone. The basic process is: fill in the details, pass the phone to the signer, and let each person sign for themselves.
**Slide 2**
Before you begin, remember three rules. Use your AirAsia Google account. Never sign for another person. Start a new form for every new case.
**Slide 3**
Get the app link from your supervisor, or scan the QR code shown here. Keep the link somewhere you can find it when you need it.
**Slide 4**
Open the link in Chrome on your phone. If Google asks you to sign in, choose your AirAsia account. Wait until the list of four forms appears. For quicker access next time, you can add the app to your home screen.
**Slide 5**
This is the home screen. You can see four forms. Before you tap anything, think about the task you are recording.
**Slide 6**
Choose FOCA for a flight security checklist. Choose NOTOC for a firearm record. Choose Acknowledgement of Receipt when items are handed over. Choose Search List when recording a search of a person, vehicle, or locker.
**Slide 7**
Let us start with FOCA. Security staff complete the checklist. The Airline Representative enters their own name and ID, then signs. The Security Representative enters their own name and ID, signs, and saves the record.
**Slide 8**
On the home screen, tap FOCA Checklist. Check the title at the top so you know you opened the right form.
**Slide 9**
The FOCA form is longer than one phone screen. Scroll down to see all of it. Flight information is at the top, checklist items are below it, and the Next button is at the bottom.
**Slide 10**
Enter the flight number, aircraft registration, destination, date, times, and aircraft bay. Check the information before moving on. Flight number and date must be entered.
**Slide 11**
Read each checklist line. Select Completed or Not Checked. Add a remark when an item needs an explanation. Continue through the additional services section as well.
**Slide 12**
Now review the form. Check the flight information and each checklist answer. If something is wrong, correct it now. When everything is ready, tap Next.
**Slide 13**
Give the phone to the Airline Representative. They tap the orange button that says, "Airline Representative has the phone." Do not complete this step for them.
**Slide 14**
The Airline Representative reviews the form before signing. If they find an error, they tap "Something is wrong" and give the phone back to Security staff for correction.
**Slide 15**
The Airline Representative types their own name and ID or staff number. They should check the spelling and number before signing.
**Slide 16**
The Airline Representative signs in the box with a finger or stylus. This must be their own signature, drawn by them on the phone.
**Slide 17**
If the signature is unclear or incorrect, tap "Clear and sign again." This removes the drawing from that signature box.
**Slide 18**
The signature box is now empty. The Airline Representative draws their signature again and checks that it looks right.
**Slide 19**
When the name, ID, and signature are correct, the Airline Representative taps "Confirm my signature." They then give the phone back to Security staff.
**Slide 20**
When the phone is back with Security staff, tap the blue button that says, "Security staff has the phone." The final FOCA signature screen will open.
**Slide 21**
The Security Representative enters their own name and ID or staff number. Check these details carefully. Do not enter the Airline Representative's details in these boxes.
**Slide 22**
The Security Representative signs in their signature box. If the signature needs correcting, tap "Clear and sign again," then redraw it.
**Slide 23**
When the form is ready, tap Save record once. The button changes to "Saving..." while the app works. Wait for the result.
**Slide 24**
Look for the green tick, the word "Saved," and a Record ID. These show that the form was saved. Do not close the page before you see this confirmation. The Record ID shown in this guide is only a test example.
**Slide 25**
After saving, tap "Open the completed PDF." The signed PDF opens in Google Drive.
**Slide 26**
Check the PDF before sharing it. Make sure it is the correct form and case. Check the file name, details, and signatures.
**Slide 27**
If an authorised person needs the PDF, tap Share in Google Drive. Take a moment to check who should receive it.
**Slide 28**
You can tap Copy link, but keep the file access set to Restricted. Send the link only to an authorised person. A copied link does not automatically give someone access. If they cannot open the PDF, ask the file owner or your supervisor to arrange access. Never change the setting to "Anyone with the link."
**Slide 29**
Next is the NOTOC Firearm form. Security staff enter the flight and firearm information. The Captain reviews and signs. The Security staff member named "Loaded by" then signs and saves the record.
**Slide 30**
Return to the app home screen and tap NOTOC Firearm. Check that you opened the correct form before entering details.
**Slide 31**
Enter the departure or arrival station, flight number, date, and aircraft registration. Use the information for the actual flight.
**Slide 32**
Complete one line for each firearm. Enter its serial number, pistol, magazine, and number of rounds. Select Gunlocker or Gunbox as appropriate. Review every line carefully.
**Slide 33**
Complete the "Loaded by" details. Then tap Next and give the phone to the Captain. The Captain must review the information before signing.
**Slide 34**
The Captain checks the NOTOC and signs for themselves. The phone then returns to Security staff. Security staff sign, tap Save once, and wait for the Saved screen. If a signature is wrong, use "Clear and sign again."
**Slide 35**
Now let us look at Acknowledgement of Receipt. This form records items being handed over. Security staff enter the details, then each named person signs for themselves.
**Slide 36**
From the app home screen, tap Acknowledgement of Receipt. Check the form title before you begin.
**Slide 37**
Complete the recipient details shown on the screen. These include the title, name, PP or NRIC, staff ID, contact number, and date. Check the details with the recipient.
**Slide 38**
List the items being received. Enter one item in each box. There is space for up to ten items. Describe each item clearly so the completed PDF is easy to understand.
**Slide 39**
Complete the handing-over staff and witness details shown on the form. Review the information, then tap Continue to signatures. Pass the phone to each named signer when their step appears.
**Slide 40**
The recipient signs for themselves. The handing-over staff member signs for themselves. The witness signs at their step when included. After the required signatures are complete, save the record and wait for Saved.
**Slide 41**
The fourth form is Search List. Security staff enter the search information. Each named person signs for themselves. A witness signature is optional.
**Slide 42**
From the home screen, tap Search List. Check that the Search List form has opened.
**Slide 43**
Enter the date, time, and place of the search. Select what was searched: a person, vehicle, or locker. Use the details from the actual search.
**Slide 44**
Complete the details of the person searched and any items found. If nothing was found, enter "Nil" in the items field, as shown in the guide.
**Slide 45**
Enter the Security staff details. Select the witness option only if a witness is involved and will sign. Check the form, then tap Continue to signatures.
**Slide 46**
Pass the phone to each named signer. Each person signs for themselves. If the witness option was selected, the witness signs too. When all required signatures are complete, save the form and wait for Saved.
**Slide 47**
Where do the PDFs go? Each completed form becomes its own PDF in Google Drive. The restricted AA-SEC Signed Forms area has separate folders for FOCA, NOTOC, Acknowledgement, and Search List. Open these folders only if your role gives you access.
**Slide 48**
Each saved form also creates a row in its records Google Sheet. There are separate records Sheets for the four forms. Authorised supervisors and staff use these Sheets to review submissions. The example row in this slide is a test record.
**Slide 49**
For a new case, start a new form. Every save creates a separate PDF and record row. Do not reuse a previous case's form.
**Slide 50**
Before you finish, check five things. Did you choose the correct form? Are the details correct? Did each person sign for themselves? Did you see the Saved screen and open the PDF? Are you sharing it only with an authorised person? If all five are correct, the process is complete.
**Slide 51**
Let us practise. Tell me which form you would use for a flight security checklist, a firearm record, an item handover, and a search. Remember the simple pattern: choose the correct form, enter accurate details, let each person sign for themselves, wait for Saved, and check the PDF. If something looks wrong or the Saved screen does not appear, keep the page open and ask your supervisor for help.
